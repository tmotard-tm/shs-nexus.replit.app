Artifact 5 — Day 30 Pause Toggle (LOA Queue UI)
In the LOA Recovery Queue, for each active tech record where loa_duration_days >= 30:

Display a "Pause Recovery" button that becomes active when the current date is within 7 calendar days of Day 30 (i.e., today >= loa_start_date + 23 days).
When toggled ON: set recovery_paused = true on that record, suppress vehicle recovery actions, and log a timestamped note: "Recovery paused — return confirmed within 7 days of Day 30."
When toggled OFF: revert recovery_paused = false and resume normal recovery flow.
Surface the pause state visibly in the queue row (e.g., a "Paused" status badge).
Artifact 6 — LOA Extension Re-trigger Logic
When a tech record is updated and loa_duration_days changes from a value under 30 to 30 or more:

Immediately trigger the 30+ day actions as if it were Day 1 of that LOA:
Fire an updated Artifact 1 email to Fleet/Assets noting the extension and the new expected return date.
Queue vehicle recovery and P-card suspension actions in the asset checklist.
Log a timestamped note on the tech record: "LOA extended past 30 days — 30-day recovery actions initiated on {today}."
Do not re-send the technician email (Artifact 2) or SMS (Artifact 4) on extension — those were already sent at LOA start.
