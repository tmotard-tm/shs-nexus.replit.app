Here are four recommendations to consider before you lock in the canonical model:

1. The "Ghost of Previous Data" (Mismatch Resolution)
When the audit in Step 2 surfaces a mismatch (e.g., Holman says the odometer is 85,000 but AMS says 84,200), the UI needs a way to "Force Sync."

The Idea: Don't just show the mismatch; provide a one-click "Master Sync" button. If the user knows Holman is right, clicking a small "Set as Source" icon next to the Holman value should automatically push that value to all other systems.

Why: It transforms the card from a "viewing tool" into a "data-cleaning tool."

2. Time-Stamped "Last Seen" Indicators
Since you are pulling from Snowflake and other async sources, "Review" data can sometimes be stale.

The Idea: Next to the Review fields, include a tiny relative timestamp (e.g., "Snowflake: 4h ago").

Why: If a dispatcher is looking at a truck's location or odometer, they need to know if they are looking at "live" data or "yesterday's" data before they make an assignment.

3. Smart "Update" Prioritization
The Update section could get cluttered if it shows every editable field for every vehicle.

The Idea: Use "Predictive Fields." If a vehicle is currently in a "Rental" status, the Update section should prioritize fields like Rental End Date or Vendor Contact. If it’s a "Fleet" vehicle, it should prioritize Odometer and Next PM (Preventative Maintenance).

Why: It keeps the "Update" region compact and contextually relevant.

4. Direct "Log Activity" in the Unassign Flow
Currently, unassigning is often a "destructive" or "binary" action (it's just gone).

The Idea: Add a "Reason for Unassignment" dropdown (e.g., Resignation, Vehicle Repair, Termination) directly in the Unassign region.

Why: This data is gold for long-term supply chain analytics. If you capture why vehicles are being unassigned now, you can later build reports on vehicle downtime or technician turnover trends without needing a separate audit.

Suggested Addition to "Done Looks Like":
Conflict Resolution UI: A clear visual pattern for resolving data mismatches between systems (e.g., Holman vs. AMS) with a "Push to All" action.

Source Freshness: Every consolidated field in the Review section displays the "Last Updated" timestamp from its source-of-truth integration.
