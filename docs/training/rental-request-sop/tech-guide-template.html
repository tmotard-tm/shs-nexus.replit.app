1. Overview

The rental request form is a single mobile-friendly page where a technician asks Fleet for a rental vehicle — either a new rental (van is down, or a new hire has no van yet) or an extension (more time on a rental they already hold). It replaces calling Holman — use this form instead.

  • One permanent link: https://SHS-Nexus.replit.app/rental-request — no login, no app. It works from a phone standing next to a dead van. You prove who you are by entering your LDAP; the form checks it against the technician roster.
  • Save the link. It never expires — bookmark it on your phone. Fleet's reminder and decision texts also include it when you need it.
  • One request at a time: the form refuses a second request while one is already open, and allows one filed request per day. If you already have a request in, contact Fleet instead of filing another.
  • Nothing is booked by the form itself. Submitting sends your request to Fleet for review. You will get the decision by text at the mobile number you confirm on the form.

2. Requesting a NEW rental — step by step

1
Open the link and enter your LDAP. Type your LDAP (e.g. JSMITH1) and press Start. If the LDAP is not on the roster the form says so — check spelling, or contact Fleet if you just started.
Fig. 1 — The landing screen. LDAP only; no password.
2
Confirm your identity. The form shows what Fleet has on record — name, truck, district, state, mobile. If it is right, tap Correct. If anything is wrong, tap Something's wrong and fix the truck number or mobile number (10 digits), or explain in the box. The mobile number matters most — every decision arrives by text at this number.
Fig. 2 — Identity check + request type choice.
Fig. 3 — "Something's wrong": edit truck / mobile, or explain.
3
Pick what you need. The form pre-selects based on Fleet's records (if records show you already hold a rental, it defaults to Extension). You can override it — records can lag — but the form will ask for one line explaining why.
4
Answer the questions. The table below lists every question and what a good answer looks like. Fig. 4 shows a fully completed example.
QuestionWhat is expected
Are you 21 years of age or older?Enterprise will not rent to drivers under 21. Answering No stops the form — contact Holman (ARI) instead; they can book through Avis or Hertz.
What issues are you having with your work van?Pick the closest match: New hire (no van yet) · Breakdown (won't run/drive, or decommissioned) · Accident or collision · Scheduled maintenance. Accident adds an "Are you okay?" question — if you are hurt, call 911 first. This form can wait.
Is your van being towed?Yes / No. Skipped for new hires.
Describe the issueYour own words, as much detail as possible: what happened, when, any warning lights, what you already tried. This is what Fleet reads first.
Repair shop — name, street, city, state, phoneThis one needs to be right. Fleet calls the shop to check on your van. Use the shop's real business phone (10 digits).
Date your van goes into the shopYour rental starts on this date — not before.
Which Enterprise location?The branch where the reservation is made and where you pick the rental up. Google "Enterprise Rent-A-Car near me" and copy the name + street. No airport branches if avoidable. New hires answer this plus their first day on the road instead of the shop questions.
AcknowledgementsOne combined checkbox for the request statements (not scheduled maintenance; van cannot be driven safely; return within 1 working day of van completion; information accurate) plus four individual terms: working-hours use only, return before 3+ days off, request an extension every 7 days, discipline up to termination for misuse. All boxes are required and recorded with the request.
Fig. 4 — A completed new-rental request (breakdown at a shop), ready to submit.
After submit
You will see "Request received — Fleet has your request and will review it. You will get a text as soon as it is decided." No confirmation number yet: booking only happens after Fleet approves.

3. Requesting an EXTENSION — step by step

An extension is more time on the rental you already have — never a different vehicle. Policy requires an extension request every 7 days while you hold a rental. Fleet's reminder text links straight here. Steps 1–2 (LDAP + identity) are identical; the form then shows the rental Fleet believes you hold and asks five questions about the repair.

QuestionWhat is expected
Your current rental (shown, not asked)Vehicle, vendor, start date, days so far, extensions so far, location — from Fleet's records. If this is not the rental you hold, say so in the first answer box.
What is the current status of your van's repair?Where the van is and what state it is in — e.g. "Still at the shop, waiting on a transmission part."
When did you last speak with the shop?A date. Fleet expects technicians to be checking on their own van — "last week" answers get called.
What did the shop say?Their actual words — e.g. "waiting on a transmission part, due Thursday."
When does the shop expect the van to be done? (optional)Date, if the shop gave one.
Roughly how much longer do you need the rental?Plain estimate — "about one more week."
AcknowledgementsThe full set again. Extensions re-sign every term, every week — that is deliberate.
Fig. 5 — A completed extension request, showing the current-rental card and repair-status answers.
After submit
"Request received" plus "Keep the rental until you hear from us." Keep driving the rental — an extension request is never a reason to return it.

4. Messages you may see

SituationMessage and what to do
LDAP not on rosterWe could not find that LDAP on the technician roster. Check the spelling, or contact Fleet if you have just started.
Already filed today (daily cap)You have already filed a rental request today and Fleet is working it. Contact Fleet directly if something has changed. Do not try to file again — call/text Fleet.
A request is already openYou already have rental request #<no> with us (<status>). Fleet is working it. Contact Fleet rather than starting a second one.
Under 21Enterprise does not rent to drivers under 21… Contact Holman (ARI) instead. They are the only ones who can put you in a rental, through Avis or Hertz. The request is recorded, closed, and Fleet is notified.
Texted link invalidThis link is invalid or has expired. Use the permanent link instead: …/rental-request.
Fleet needs more info (send-back text)Sears Fleet: we cannot approve rental request #… yet because we still need <items>. Finish it here: <link> Your earlier answers are saved. Reopen the link — earlier answers are pre-filled; only the missing items are asked.
